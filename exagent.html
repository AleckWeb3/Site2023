Workflow Automation

Exagent

Intelligent workflow orchestration that connects your tools and automates repetitive processes — freeing your team to focus on higher-value work.

What Exagent Does

Exagent connects your existing tools — email, CRMs, databases, document systems, and APIs — into automated workflows. Instead of manually moving data between systems, you define the logic once and let Exagent handle the routing, transformation, and handoffs.

🔗 Multi-Tool Connectors

Pre-built connectors for common business tools. Connect your stack without writing custom integration code.

🔄 Conditional Routing

Define rules-based workflows: "If this invoice is over $5K, route it to manager approval. If approved, push to accounting."

📊 Process Visibility

Track every workflow run. See what triggered, what completed, what's waiting, and where bottlenecks are forming.

🧩 Human-in-the-Loop Steps

When a decision needs human input, Exagent pauses the workflow and notifies the right person — with full context.

Example Use Cases

📬 Invoice Processing

Invoice arrives via email → extracted by Apex AI → Exagent routes to approver → pushes to accounting system → archives document. All tracked.

👋 Client Onboarding

New client form submitted → Exagent creates CRM record → triggers welcome email → assigns onboarding tasks → notifies account manager.

📋 Compliance Reviews

Document processed → Exagent checks against rules → routes to compliance officer if flagged → logs approval → stores audit trail.

🔄 Cross-System Sync

Data entered in one system → Exagent transforms and syncs to another → validates both sides match → alerts if discrepancies found.

How It Works with Apex AI

Exagent pairs naturally with Apex AI. Apex AI handles document processing and extraction — Exagent takes those results and routes them through your workflows. Together, they form a complete automation pipeline from document intake to downstream action.

But Exagent also works standalone with any tool that has an API, a webhook, or email integration.
