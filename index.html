Frequently Asked Questions

What does Web Three Innovations do?

We build AI-powered software products for operations teams. Our current products are Apex AI (document review automation) and Exagent (workflow orchestration). Each product solves a specific operational problem with auditable AI assistance — not black-box automation.

Is my data used to train your AI models?

No. Customer data is never used to train shared models. Your data is isolated to your organization. Read more in our Security Policy.

Can I try before buying?

Yes. We offer pilot programs where you can test our products with your own documents and workflows. Contact us to get started.

How do I choose between Apex AI and Exagent?

Apex AI is for document review and extraction. Exagent is for connecting tools and automating workflows. Many teams use both — Apex to process documents, Exagent to route the results into their existing systems.
