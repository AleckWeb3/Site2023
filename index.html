Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of documents can Apex AI process?

Apex AI is designed for structured and semi-structured business documents: contracts, invoices, compliance reports, vendor agreements, and operational forms. It works best with documents that follow predictable patterns.

How accurate is the AI?

Accuracy varies by document type and task. We measure and publish accuracy metrics per task category. For tasks where the AI is uncertain, it flags items for human review rather than guessing. We encourage customers to test with their own documents before deployment.

Does your AI train on my documents?

No. Customer documents are not used to train or improve shared AI models. Your data is isolated to your organization.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on document volume and use case. We offer transparent per-document or subscription pricing. Contact us for a quote based on your needs. No hidden fees.

Can I try it before committing?

Yes. We offer a pilot program where you can test Apex AI with your own documents and workflows. No upfront commitment required.
