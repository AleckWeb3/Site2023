Sales Automation

Apex AI

A top-of-funnel sales automation workspace that researches target companies, qualifies them, prepares approved outreach, handles replies, and delivers qualified leads — all under your control.

What Apex AI Does

Apex AI turns target companies into qualified leads through one continuous, controlled workflow. Instead of manually researching companies, finding contacts, drafting outreach, tracking replies, and handing off leads — you define the rules once and Apex handles the routine work while you stay in control.

🎯 Company Research & Fit

Researches target companies and applies your custom Company Fit rules — dimensions, evidence requirements, weights, thresholds, and hard exclusions. The AI explains evidence but cannot change the fit score.

👤 Contact Discovery

Finds one current, suitable contact in an approved buyer role with a valid business email. If it can't verify a suitable person, Apex tries another approved role or defers the company.

✉️ Outreach Preparation

Prepares first and follow-up messages under your approved sales plan and Message guidance. You can edit any message or request a new version. Normal work doesn't require repeated approval.

📬 Reply Handling

Checks replies, classifies useful reply types, handles opt-outs and do-not-contact requests, and stops follow-up after a reply — so you never chase someone who already responded.

✅ Qualified Lead Delivery

Prepares qualified leads for human distribution. You download a CSV or JSON file and import it into your existing sales system. Apex does not route leads among your reps.

🛡️ Full Control

You approve the complete sales plan once, then pause or stop outreach anytime. Material changes need your review. Independent sending controls, schedules, limits, and suppression rules stay in force.

How It Works

The dashboard has six areas: Overview, Accounts, Outreach, Inbox, Qualified Leads, and Settings. Overview tracks eight stages from target company to qualified lead handoff.

1

Set Up Your Sales Plan

Define your company and offer, target rules, exclusions, buyer roles, Message guidance, sample messages, follow-up plans, email connection, sending days and limits, and qualified-lead handoff. This is done once per campaign — not per company.

2

Add Target Companies

Add one company, upload a target-company list, or use targets Apex finds under your active sales plan.

3

Research & Company Fit

Apex researches each company, records its sources, and applies your Company Fit method. Missing, stale, or conflicting evidence produces defined results — the AI never silently changes the score.

4

Find Contacts

Apex looks for one current contact in an approved buyer role with a valid business email. If it can't verify a suitable person, it tries another approved role within your limit or defers the company.

5

Prepare & Approve Messages

Apex prepares first and follow-up messages under your sales plan and Message guidance. You can edit any message or request a new version. Only true exceptions need your review.

6

Approve & Start Outreach

Use "Approve and start outreach" once to activate the complete plan. Outreach runs through Microsoft 365 or Classic Outlook with independent sending controls, schedules, limits, pause, stop, and suppression rules.

7

Handle Replies

Apex checks replies, classifies useful reply types, handles opt-outs and do-not-contact requests, and stops follow-up after a reply.

8

Receive Qualified Leads

Apex prepares qualified leads. You download a CSV or JSON file and import it into your sales system. Your sales reps receive leads — they don't use Apex.

What Apex AI Is — And Isn't

Apex AI Is

  • A top-of-funnel automation workspace for a Sales Director or program owner
  • One continuous workflow from target company to qualified-lead handoff
  • Controlled by you — one approved sales plan, with pause/stop anytime
  • Built with isolated client environments and audited access
  • Integrated with Microsoft 365 and Classic Outlook email

Apex AI Is Not

  • A full CRM or pipeline management tool
  • A tool that sells to or replies to prospects
  • A lead routing system for sales representatives
  • A dashboard used by everyday sales reps
  • A direct integration with HubSpot or other CRMs (currently)
  • A document review or data extraction tool

Trust & Architecture

🔐 Isolated Environments

Each client has its own application service, database, secrets, files, jobs, caches, logs, and audit trail. No cross-client data access.

👤 Controlled Access

Separate platform and client sessions. Named client accounts, encrypted connection keys, and audited support entry. Platform roles: Owner, Administrator, and Observer. Observers cannot enter client environments.

📝 Audit Trail

Platform audit events, tenant-local audit trails, and audited staff entry. Records for access grants, revocations, sensitive activity, lifecycle actions, and sales-development events.

🛡️ Data Controls

Retention, suppression, privacy, and legal-hold controls. Critical authorization, review, and audit evidence is protected from normal deletion.
