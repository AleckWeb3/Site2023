Sales Automation

Apex AI

Top-of-funnel sales automation that researches target companies, qualifies them against your rules, prepares approved outreach, handles replies, and delivers qualified leads — all under your control.

How it works

Eight stages from target company to qualified lead handoff. One continuous, controlled workflow.

01 Setup

Sales Plan Configuration

Define company and offer, target rules, buyer roles, Message guidance, follow-up plans, email connection, sending limits, and lead handoff — once per campaign.

→
02 Input

Add Target Companies

Add one company, upload a list, or let Apex find targets under your active sales plan.

→
03 Research

Company Fit Analysis

Research each company, record sources, and apply your Company Fit rules. Dimensions, weights, thresholds, and hard exclusions — deterministic scoring.

→
04 Contact

Contact Discovery

Find one current contact in an approved role with valid email. If unverifiable, try another role within limits or defer the company.

→
05 Message

Outreach Preparation

Prepare first and follow-up messages under your sales plan. Edit any message or request a new version. Only true exceptions need your review.

→
06 Launch

Approve & Start

One approval activates the complete plan. Outreach runs through Microsoft 365 or Classic Outlook with independent sending controls, schedules, and limits.

→
07 Reply

Handle Replies

Classify replies, handle opt-outs, stop follow-up after response. Never chase someone who already engaged.

→
08 Deliver

Qualified Lead Handoff

Download CSV or JSON and import into your sales system. Your reps receive leads — they don't use Apex.

Architecture

Built for trust and isolation.

Isolated Environments

Each client has a dedicated application service, database, secrets vault, jobs, caches, logs, and audit trail.

Controlled Access

Separate platform and client sessions. Platform roles: Owner, Administrator, Observer. Observers cannot enter client environments.

Audit Trail

Records for access grants, revocations, sensitive activity, lifecycle actions, and sales-development events. Critical evidence protected from normal deletion.
