3. Engineering Principles

  1. Controlled AI — Products never take autonomous action on your behalf. They suggest, flag, and escalate — you approve or override every decision with full audit trails.
  2. Data Isolation — Your data is never used to train shared models. Each client environment is fully isolated with encrypted storage and strict access controls. No cross-tenant contamination — your IT team retains full governance over data and infrastructure boundaries.
  3. Iterative Delivery — Ship early with core functionality and improve based on real usage. Roadmap driven by actual user feedback, not speculative feature lists.
